About

An India-based studio, building for Europe.

DEVLOK is a software development studio focused on building digital products and business solutions for European businesses.

Who we are

A small, independent studio operating from India. We don't have a European office or a long client list, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we have is working products you can see.

Who we work with

Growing SMEs, founders and decision-makers in Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden who need software that fits how they actually work.

Philosophy

Understand the problem first, then design, build and refine. Clear scope, clean interfaces, honest communication.

Technology

Modern, maintainable web technologies: React, TypeScript, and the backend and database that fit the job.

Long-term vision

To grow from showcase products into a trusted partner for European businesses, with a professional presence, more delivered projects and more languages.
